Pico Bricks — every module over OSC

Live values sit under the part they come from, and the actuators are the badges: the RGB LED opens a colour picker, the relay toggles, the buzzer plays. Everything arrives in one /pb message so the readings share an instant. Web Serial needs http://localhost. Note: this is Robotistan's stock photo — a v2.1 board like yours carries an SHTC3 on I²C where the picture shows a blue DHT11, and its motor driver is an I²C slave rather than direct GPIO.
