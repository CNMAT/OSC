T-Display-S3 — draw by OSC, touch back

The board's 320×170 screen, mirrored below at 2×. Draw on the mirror and the strokes go to the real panel as /display messages; touch the real glass and the board's /state + /touch bundles light up here in green. The dot the board draws under your finger is the map check — if it tracks, the landscape transform is right; if not, flip the toggles. Web Serial needs http://localhost, Chrome or Edge.
