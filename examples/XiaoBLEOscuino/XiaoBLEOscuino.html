XiaoBLEOscuino

The same SLIP-framed OSC 1.0 this repo puts on a wire, carried over Bluetooth LE instead — Nordic UART Service, no gateway, no server. Pair with XiaoBLEOscuino.ino on a XIAO nRF52840 Sense.
Not connected
Args are typed automatically: 1 → int, 0.5 → float. Messages are sent bare (not bundled) because this sketch reads either. BLE writes are chunked to 20 bytes, the conservative NUS payload — SLIP does not care where the chunk boundaries fall, which is the point of framing.
