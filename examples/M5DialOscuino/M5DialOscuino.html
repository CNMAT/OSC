M5Dial — knob, touch and round screen over OSC

The Dial's round 240×240 screen mirrored at 2×, wrapped in a ring that tracks the knob. Draw on the mirror and it lands on the glass as /display/circle or /display/rect; turn the knob, press the G0 button or touch the screen and a /state bundle comes back carrying /enc, /btn and, while a finger is down, /touch. The rate slider sets /rate (0 stops the stream; the board echoes the value in force) and "read" asks /enc and /btn once. Connecting asks /enq; the reply lists what the board proved it has as /enq lines. Web Serial needs http://localhost, Chrome or Edge.
