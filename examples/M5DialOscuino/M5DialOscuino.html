M5Dial — knob, touch and round screen over OSC

The Dial's round 240×240 screen mirrored at 2×, wrapped in a ring that tracks the knob. Draw on the mirror and it lands on the glass as /disp messages; turn the knob, press the G0 button or touch the screen and /dial events come back. Web Serial needs http://localhost, Chrome or Edge.
