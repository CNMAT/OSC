EsploraOscuino

The entire Esplora state arrives as one OSC bundle — /state plus /btn, /joy, /pot, /light, /temp, /mic and /imu, sampled in one pass — the capability-named address space of ADDRESSES.md, so nothing on the wire is named after the board. Sent on change, plus a heartbeat so silence still means something. Chrome or Edge, over Web Serial.
