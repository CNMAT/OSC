The board answers /enq with its name and one /enq/<capability> line per thing it actually has; a panel below appears for each one announced. A stream is a bundle of /state <seq> <millis> plus one message per streamed capability, every /rate ms (0 stops it). Over HTTP the page polls GET /state at that period instead.

Args are typed automatically: 1 → int, 0.5 → float, anything else → string. Force a type with i: f: s:, or use bare T F N. bundle is ticked by default because PlaygroundOscuino.ino reads bundles, as do the stock Oscuino examples and the CNMAT Max patches.

Adafruit Circuit Playground Express. A1-A7 are the alligator-clip pads. D4/D5 are the buttons and D7 the slide switch, all read with /u for the internal pullup.
