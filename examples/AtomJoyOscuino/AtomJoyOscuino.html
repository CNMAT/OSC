M5Stack Atom JoyStick — sticks, buttons and screen over OSC

Both Hall sticks, the shoulder buttons, the stick clicks, the AtomS3's front button and its 128×128 screen, as one bundle per instant: /state, /joy (four axes), /btn (five buttons) and /bat (both cells). Connecting asks /enq; the reply lists what is really there as /enq lines. The sticks live behind an STM32 co-processor at I²C 0x59, not on the ESP32's ADC. Type on the emulated glass and it goes to the real LCD as /display/text. Web Serial needs http://localhost.
