PyBadge M4 Express

Eight buttons, the light sensor, the battery divider and the LIS3DH arrive as one bundle every /rate ms, 50 by default — /state, /btn, /light, /bat and /imu — sampled in one pass so every reading belongs to the same instant. The buttons come back as eight ints, one per button, read on the badge through a 74HC165 shift register. Ask any of those addresses with no arguments and it answers once, on the same address, without waiting for the stream. Going the other way, the page drives the 160×128 screen (/display), the DAC speaker (/buzz), all five NeoPixels (/rgb) and the red LED (/s/l); every write is echoed back, so the readouts here follow the board rather than the click. On connect it asks /enq; the accelerometer and microphone panels follow whether /enq/imu and /enq/mic are in the answer.

EdgeBadge PDM microphone, on the same /rate tick as the stream bundle. rms and peak arrive at full scale, 0–32767, after the sketch's MIC_GAIN — so they are gain-dependent, and CLIP lights when a transient pins 32767. Measured on this badge at MIC_GAIN 16, corrected for the rms scaling bug those captures predate: a quiet room floors near −34 dBFS, rising about 26.6 dB to the loudest frame. At the shipped gain of 8 the floor should sit roughly 6 dB lower again — arithmetic, not a fresh capture.
