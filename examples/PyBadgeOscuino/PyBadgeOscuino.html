PyBadge M4 Express

Eight buttons, the light sensor, the battery divider and the LIS3DH arrive as a single /pybadge message, sampled in one pass so every reading belongs to the same instant. The buttons come back as one bitmask, because on this board they are read through a 74HC165 shift register. Going the other way, the page drives the 160×128 screen, the DAC speaker and all five NeoPixels.
